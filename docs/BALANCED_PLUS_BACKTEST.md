# Balanced Plus: rolling historical backtest

> Generated by `scripts/backtest/make-backtest-doc.ts` from `docs/backtest/balanced-plus-backtest-results.json`. Re-run `npm run backtest:bp` then `npx tsx scripts/backtest/make-backtest-doc.ts` to reproduce. Audit that preceded this work: [BALANCED_PLUS_AUDIT.md](BALANCED_PLUS_AUDIT.md).

## Recommendation

**Balanced Plus is competitive, but no advantage over Balanced is established.**

- Held-out seasons 2024-25 and 2025-26: 58 rolling five-gameweek windows (12 non-overlapping).
- Balanced Plus minus Balanced: mean +5.6 points per window (median -1.5, SD 33.6); 95% CI -19.7 to +26.0 (t, non-overlapping) and -2.1 to +15.3 (block bootstrap).
- By season: 2024-25 +15.9, 2025-26 -4.6. Development season 2023-24: +19.8 (not used as evidence).

Why, in short:

- The pooled held-out point estimate favours Balanced Plus (**+5.6 points per five-gameweek window**, ≈ +1.1 per gameweek), but **both 95% intervals are consistent with no difference** (t: [-19.7, +26.0]; block bootstrap: [-2.1, +15.3]), the **median is -1.5** (a few large windows drive the mean), the sign test is far from significant (7 wins / 5 losses, p = 0.77), and the **two held-out seasons disagree in sign** (2024-25: +15.9, 2025-26: -4.6).
- The development season showed +19.8 per window. The held-out seasons show much less. A large effect that shrinks out of sample is what chance plus a small sample looks like; it is not evidence of a durable edge.
- The evidence standard: the backtest code (fixed before the run) labels a difference as evidence only if **both** 95% intervals exclude zero. The verdict logic added afterwards, when this report was written, also requires the same sign in every held-out season; that extra clause cannot change this verdict because the first condition already fails. Balanced Plus does not meet the standard. It also does not underperform: its average rank (2.34) equals Balanced's (2.35).
- **Statistical power is the limiting factor.** With 12 independent windows and a window-difference SD of 33.6, the study can only detect a difference of about **29 points per window (≈ 5.8 per gameweek)** with 80% power. Detecting a real 2-points-per-gameweek edge would need on the order of **89 independent windows**, about 15 seasons of non-overlapping windows.

## Methodology

**Question.** Does the Balanced Plus selection process produce more actual FPL points than Balanced, Safe and Aggressive when each is applied only with information available at the time?

**Unit of evaluation: a five-gameweek window.** At the deadline of gameweek *k* each strategy builds a legal 15-man squad (£100m, max 3 per club, 2/5/5/3) from point-in-time data using its **own** selection rule (Balanced, Safe, Aggressive: the unchanged optimiser; Balanced Plus: its own solver). The squad is held for GW *k…k+4* (no transfers, chips or price changes). At every gameweek the strategy's own lineup rule re-selects XI, bench order, captain and vice from data available at **that** gameweek's deadline (Balanced/Safe/Aggressive: the unchanged `bestLineup`; Balanced Plus: its XI rule with the 2-per-club cap and secure-starter bars, relaxing them in a fixed order only if the squad cannot satisfy them, which happened in 1.7% of gameweeks). Actual points then apply FPL's automatic substitutions and the vice-captain rule.

**Windows.** *k* = 6…34 per season (≥ 5 gameweeks of history; ≥ 5 gameweeks left): 29 rolling windows per season, of which 6 share no gameweek. All four strategies are scored on exactly the same windows.

**Splits and no tuning.**

| Role | Season | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Development | 2023-24 | verify the harness only; no parameter was chosen from it |
| **Held-out evaluation** | **2024-25, 2025-26** | **the conclusion** |
| Attempted, excluded | 2022-23 | rejected by the data-quality gate (below) |
| Not used | 2026-27 (live) | only 1 usable window (10 gameweeks played; windows need 5 prior and 5 following gameweeks) |

Strategy code and parameters are frozen (`data/backtest-frozen.json`: SHA-256 of the optimiser, projection engine and Balanced Plus source plus its parameters) and the backtest refuses to run if they change. **No strategy weight or threshold was tuned against any window.** The sensitivity analysis below is descriptive.

**Statistics.**

- Paired differences (strategy minus Balanced) on window totals.
- Overlapping windows are serially dependent, so inference uses (a) a **t interval on the non-overlapping windows** and (b) a **moving-block bootstrap (blocks of 5 windows, 5,000 resamples, fixed seed) on all rolling windows**; plus an exact sign test on the non-overlapping windows. Effect size = mean difference / SD.
- "Evidence" is claimed only if both intervals exclude zero. Everything else is labelled *no evidence of difference*, which is **not** the same as *evidence of no difference*.
- Rotation split, formation tables, captain and bench metrics are **descriptive** (heuristic reading, not tested).

**2022-23 exclusion.** The source has **no `starts` for GW1-15 and no xG/xA for GW1-14**, so a start probability and attacking rates cannot be formed. An automatic gate (every earlier gameweek must carry starts for ≥ 80% of 60+ minute rows and xG/xA for ≥ 40% of played rows) rejected every window. Before the gate existed, Balanced Plus found *no legal squad at all* in 2022-23 (no player passed the secure-starter bar), which is how the problem was found. The data was not patched. Also GW7 is absent from that season.

### Point-in-time construction and leakage controls

- Squads are chosen at deadline k from rows with round < k only; lineups at each later gameweek use rows with round < that gameweek.
- Prices are the previous gameweek's price (up to ~7 days stale); the £100m budget and 3-per-club limit apply to every strategy.
- Injury/suspension/availability news has no point-in-time source and is NOT used. A proxy from past minutes only (0 minutes in each of a club's last two matches => doubtful) is applied equally to all strategies; Stage C removes it.
- The fixture list is the FINAL season schedule, so fixtures rearranged after an earlier deadline appear in their final gameweek. FDR is not used.
- A player with no row before the deadline is unknown at that deadline (new signings are invisible until they have a row).
- Strategies hold one squad for five gameweeks (no transfers, chips, or price changes) and re-pick XI/bench/captain at each gameweek with their own lineup rule. FPL auto-substitutions and the vice-captain rule are applied.
- Ownership % is derived as selected / (sum of selected / 15) of the previous gameweek.
- `form` is derived as the mean points of the last three appearances.
- Source data is a third-party scrape (vaastav/Fantasy-Premier-League); completeness and accuracy are assumed, not verified against FPL.
- Windows containing a gameweek with no recorded fixtures are skipped.
- Parameters were NOT tuned on any window. The sensitivity runs are descriptive.

Leakage checks (automated, in `tests/balancedPlusBacktest.test.ts`):

1. Corrupting **every** row, price, ownership figure and outcome from the deadline onward leaves the point-in-time dataset byte-identical, and leaves every strategy's squad and first-gameweek lineup identical, while the scored points change.
2. Fixtures count as finished only if their gameweek precedes the deadline; prices are the previous gameweek's.
3. The three existing strategies in the harness equal a direct call of the unchanged optimiser on the same point-in-time data.
4. The availability proxy is built from past minutes only and can be switched off.

### Known contamination that cannot be removed

- **Injury/availability news** has no point-in-time source. A past-minutes proxy is used for all strategies; with the proxy off (everyone available) the conclusion is unchanged (BP +7.9, block-bootstrap CI [-1.4, +17.3]).
- The **fixture list is the final schedule** (rearranged fixtures appear in their final gameweek).
- **Prices** are up to ~7 days stale; the third-party scrape is assumed accurate.
- Players with no row before a deadline are invisible (new signings), identically for all strategies.
- Strategies hold a fixed squad: this tests selection quality, not transfer management, chips or price changes.

### Club concentration, availability and bench

- The **2-per-club rule works as designed** (mean max from one club in the XI 1.99 vs 2.62; 3+ from one club in 62% of Balanced gameweeks and 0% of Balanced Plus ones) **at no measurable cost in points**.
- The **secure-minutes rule did not measurably reduce non-playing starters in the held-out seasons** (8.0% vs 7.8%; minutes per starter 81.3 vs 80.5). In the development season it did (1.10 vs 1.28 non-playing starters per gameweek), another sign that the development effect did not carry over.
- Balanced Plus's bench adds slightly more through auto-subs (2.59 vs 2.12 pts/GW) and leaves more points unused on the bench (9.9 vs 8.7), consistent with a deliberately stronger bench.

**What is statistical evidence and what is heuristic.**

| Statistical (supports an inference about performance) | Heuristic / descriptive (does not) |
|---|---|
| paired differences, t interval on non-overlapping windows, block bootstrap, sign test, window ranks | rotation split, formation table, captain and bench metrics, ablation table |
| the stated decision rule (both intervals exclude 0, same sign in each held-out season) | every Balanced Plus threshold (75% / 60 min / 50% / bench 12 / weights), the minutes-security formula, the discount weights |
| leakage tests, frozen strategy hashes | the availability proxy and the choice of a 5-gameweek hold |

- **Discount weights** (flat, default, front-loaded): differences between them are ≈ ±2 points per window, far inside the noise. The five-gameweek weighting is not what drives results.
- **Without the minutes-security multiplier** the point estimate is *higher* (+4.6 vs default Balanced Plus), i.e. the multiplier did not help here. Not established, but no support for it.
- **Keeping form and realised goals/assists** (undoing the xG-only and form-removal ingredients) is also higher by +3.1: no support for the claim that removing them improves results.
- **Without the club cap**: -1.3: the cap is points-neutral, as above.

## Limitations

- **Two held-out seasons and 12 independent windows** is a small sample; the study cannot detect edges below roughly 5.8 points per gameweek.
- Single-hold strategy test (no transfers, chips or price changes): it says nothing about season-long play.
- No point-in-time injury news; the proxy is crude. (No point-in-time injury or availability source exists.)
- Final fixture schedule (rearranged fixtures appear in their final gameweek); stale prices (up to ~7 days); third-party data. Also 2022-23 is excluded and 2026-27 is unused.
- Selection rules were designed knowing 2026-27 data and general FPL experience; the held-out seasons are out-of-sample for the *parameters* (not tuned) but not for the *ideas*.
- The ablation analysis explores many variants (multiple-comparison risk).
